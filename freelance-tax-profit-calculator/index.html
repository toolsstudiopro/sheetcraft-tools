Frequently Asked Questions

How much should freelancers set aside for taxes?

A common rule of thumb is 25%–35% of net profit (revenue minus deductible business expenses) to cover both self-employment/social taxes and federal/state income tax.

Why shouldn't freelancers divide desired salary by 2,080 hours?

Full-time freelancers rarely bill 40 hours/week for 52 weeks. Once you subtract vacations, sick days, marketing, proposals, and admin time, realistic billable utilization is usually 60%–70%.

How does the day rate calculation work?

Your recommended Day Rate multiplies your true minimum hourly rate by 8 billable hours so every booked client day covers overhead, non-billable admin time, and taxes.
