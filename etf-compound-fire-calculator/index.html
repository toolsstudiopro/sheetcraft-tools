Frequently Asked Questions

How does monthly DCA compounding work?

Investing a fixed dollar amount every month automatically buys more shares when markets dip and compounds returns on both your principal and past gains.

Why adjust future portfolio value for inflation?

Because inflation erodes purchasing power every year, looking at Inflation-Adjusted Real Value shows what your future nest egg is truly worth in today's dollars.

What is the 4% Safe Withdrawal Rule (FIRE)?

The 4% rule estimates you can withdraw 4% of a diversified stock/bond portfolio annually (divided by 12 for monthly income) for long-term financial independence.
